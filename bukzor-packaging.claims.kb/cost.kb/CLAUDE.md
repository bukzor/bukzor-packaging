# cost.kb -- maintenance guide

Every decision in this kb is a cost/benefit claim wearing an
architectural costume. This theory keeps the arithmetic visible so a later
reader can attack the estimate rather than the conclusion.

- `prior:` `levels.kb`
- `ontology:` fixed site cost, marginal item cost, marginal benefit,
  threshold, worth-testing set, drift rate, silent failure, amortization,
  language relativity, estimate
- `defeated by:` a per-package cost that does not fall when a workspace
  already exists -- if standing up package *n*+1 costs what package 1 cost,
  the site/item split is fiction and clustering is a taste question

## What belongs here

Claims about what something costs, what it is worth, and how the answer
moves when the site changes. Estimates with their units.

## What does NOT belong here

- Whether the members belong together at all -> `../seams.kb/`. Cost
  presumes a legitimate cluster; it ranks and gates, it does not license.
- Whether anyone but the author wants it -> `../graduation.kb/`, which
  consumes this theory's threshold.

## Maintenance

- **Write the estimate, not the verdict.** "Not worth it" is unattackable;
  "20 minutes of site cost against a fact that drifts twice a year" is.
- Units are minutes, and they are guesses. Say so. A guess on the record
  beats a judgment off it.
