# levels.kb -- maintenance guide

The stratification every other theory locates its claims on: what can be
observed about a tool, at what price, and which questions that observation
can and cannot decide.

- `prior:` none -- this is the root theory
- `ontology:` tool, observation, level, cheap/costly, abstraction map,
  factoring, decidable-in-the-abstract, triage, audience, upstream fact
- `defeated by:` an audience predicate computable from file contents --
  which would collapse L3 into L1 and make the whole tower one level

## What belongs here

A claim about *what kind of observation* settles a kind of question, and
what it costs to make. Claims about the ordering itself, and about gaps
between levels.

## What does NOT belong here

- What to do with a cluster once observed -> `../seams.kb/`.
- What an observation is worth relative to its price -> `../cost.kb/`.
  The distinction: this theory says which observations *can* decide a
  question; `cost` says whether making them is worth it.
