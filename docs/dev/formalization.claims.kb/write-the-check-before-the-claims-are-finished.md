# Write the Check Before the Claims Are Finished

Carrier: a theory under construction and the cheapest measurement over its
population. Law:

> The instrument rewrites the claims. Prose cannot find what a census finds, so
> the order is population, then instrument, then laws -- and a law drafted before
> its check will be edited by it.

The failure this prevents is a theory that never met its data and reads as though
it did.

## Two instances, in both directions

- **A finding nobody drafted.** A use census, written to certify a claim about
  deletion, returned two things the draft did not contain: that the measure was
  polluted by its own analyst, and that the least-used tools were exactly the ones
  a structural check had already rejected. Two instruments sharing no input,
  agreeing.
- **A flattering law, priced.** A discipline census reported that the house rule
  held for **one** tool of twenty. The prose had been about to describe the
  population; instead the law became a design rule with a stated gap of five
  renames and three extractions.

## What it costs

An instrument built early measures the wrong thing at first, and its first output
is usually embarrassing. Both were true here, and both were cheaper than the
alternative: the census that would have been written last is the census that
would have been written to agree.

## What would kill it

A domain with no cheap instrument -- audience, taste, anything genuinely outside
the files. Then say the claims are judgments, mark them as such, and do not build
a proxy that decides what it cannot see.
