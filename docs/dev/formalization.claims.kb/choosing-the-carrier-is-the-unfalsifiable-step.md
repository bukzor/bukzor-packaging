# Choosing the Carrier Is the Unfalsifiable Step

Carrier: a formalization before any law is stated -- the choice of what the
objects are. Law:

> The carrier is fixed before the operations, the laws, and the census, and
> **no count can refute it.** A wrong carrier still admits a population, still
> satisfies laws, and still produces instances. Its only detectors are a law
> that fails on the data and a law that holds while restating a check you
> already had.

The second detector is the dangerous one, because it looks like success.
`PAYOFF`'s population bar catches the empty theory; nothing catches the theory
whose objects were the wrong objects, since being wrong about the carrier costs
you *content*, not instances.

## Smallest instance

The transposed category in `../../../bukzor-packaging.claims.md`'s killed
conjectures. Taking the artifacts as the objects made composition mean
reachability in the reference graph, which an existing script already reported.
No law broke. The laws were true and said nothing, and the fix was not a weaker
conjecture but a different carrier -- after which the same conjecture carried a
whole theory. **The reach was not too ambitious; it was transposed.**

## The diagnostic

If your laws come out true and each one restates a check you already ran,
suspect the carrier rather than the laws. Ask what the *arrows* are: a carrier
whose objects have no interesting maps between them is usually a carrier of
what you can see instead of what you can compose.

## What would kill it

A formalization where a wrong carrier was caught by counting -- the population
bar failing on the transposed version before any law was tried. Then carrier
choice is inside `PAYOFF` and needs no claim of its own.
